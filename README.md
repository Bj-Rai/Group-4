## About the Game
Mind Maze is a puzzle-based educational game where the player is trapped inside a maze. To escape, the player must unlock doors by answering questions and solving problems.

Each room in the maze has a challenge, and the player must use knowledge and logic to move forward. The game becomes more difficult as the player goes deeper into the maze.

---

## Theme
Puzzle, Educational, Dungeon Crawler

---

## Subject Integration
The game includes questions from different subjects:
- Mathematics  
- Science  
- History  
- General Logic  

It also includes basic economics through the use of in-game currency (Gold/Ter).

---

## Game Concept
The player is inside a maze with many locked doors. Each door can only be opened by solving a question or challenge.

The player must think carefully, manage resources, and decide when to use hints or power-ups. The goal is to escape the maze by unlocking all necessary doors.

---

## Learning Objectives
- Improve problem-solving and logical thinking  
- Strengthen understanding of academic subjects  
- Practice decision-making and resource management  

---

## How the Game Works
- The player starts with 5 hearts  
- Each wrong answer reduces one heart  
- If all hearts are lost, the game ends  
- The maze becomes more difficult as the player progresses  
- The player earns Gold (Ter) by answering correctly  

The player can:
- Spend gold on hints  
- Use power-ups  
- Unlock skins  

---

## Game Features

Knowledge Gates:
- Each door requires answering a question  

Health System:
- 5 hearts at the start  
- Wrong answers reduce health  

Gold (Ter) System:
- Earned through correct and fast answers  
- Used for hints and customization  

Power-Ups:
- Master Key (skip a door)  
- Extra life  

Difficulty Scaling:
- Questions become harder at higher levels
